Building beside the system

Three ways a build that lives outside the system fails.

1

Every build redoes the plumbing.

Extraction, normalisation, entity and currency mapping: done again for each build, by the one person who understood the last one. Three builds, three copies of the same data, three definitions of DSO.

2

Nothing is administered.

Who can run it, who changed it, which version produced last month's figure: nobody can say. The output is one person's number. Auditors, lenders and the board do not read one person's number.

3

The number stays outside.

Treasury does not read it, the forecast does not use it, the next close makes it stale. A model that cannot write into the cash position is an opinion, however good the logic.

The demand is real and the tooling is good. What is missing is a destination: an environment where a build becomes part of the data everyone else already works from.

  • The dated cash forecast: weeks 2 to 13 from the ledger, the next quarters from the budget, versioned at each close

What an extension does with it
  • Reads the position and the forecast, per entity and currency

  • Exports in the format of the TMS that already runs day 0 to 5
The extension is not a second forecast. It is extra flows on the one forecast treasury already reads.

What the layer holds
  • One GL import: P&L, balance sheet, direct and indirect cash flow, drillable to the entry, consolidated across entities (QuickBooks, Xero, 19 ERP layouts or a CSV)
  • Every entry carries entity, account, counterparty, cost centre and date: slice on any of them
  • Budget and a ten-lever simulator, progressively replaced by actuals as months close
  • Versions cut by the close, the previous one locked; valuation from the same inputs
What an extension does with it
  • Reads projected statements, not just cash movements: a receivables balance at end-November, a debt, a position per currency
  • Write-backFeeds its own drivers into the budget (volumes, prices, headcount from your own systems) and the forecast rolls
  • Write-backBooks provisional entries from your logic, in a suspense account until someone confirms
  • Gets the same answer at the next close: saved rules, deterministic replay, audit trail
